## Long description

Lumen Shelf tells a shop what to restock, what to move between outlets and
what is not selling, before anything runs out. It reads what is already in
ERPNext and puts everything on one page, in English and Arabic.

**Today, in plain sentences.** One page lists what needs doing now: items
running low and when they run out, stock to move to the outlet where it
sells, batches that may expire and money tied up in stock that does not
sell. Each line says what is happening, why, and what to do.

**Restocking that learns.** Sales speed per item and outlet, weighted to
recent weeks, with days out of stock left out. Lead times learned per
supplier, safety stock, reorder point and a quantity rounded to the
purchase pack and minimum order. One button turns the suggestions into
ERPNext Material Requests, grouped by supplier or sending warehouse.

**Transfers before purchases.** The main warehouse's spare stock and other
outlets' surplus cover a shortage before anything is bought. Stock that
sits in one outlet and sells in another moves to where it sells.

**A smarter forecast.** Seasons learned from your own sales on the
Gregorian or Hijri calendar, promotions from pricing rules, rising and
falling items, intermittent demand, new items judged from similar ones,
shelf life and batch expiry, shelf capacity, and a report of how accurate
the forecast was.

**Automatic mode.** Optional: keeps ERPNext's own reorder levels up to date
every night, and ERPNext raises the material requests.

**Ideas.** Items that sell in other outlets but are missing from one, items
bought together, and, with Lumen's optional AI service, trending products
in your country and ready to sell bundles with a price that keeps your
margin.

Works on ERPNext v13 to v16, with POS Invoices and Sales Invoices. Every
feature has its own switch, and access follows ERPNext roles and user
permissions.
